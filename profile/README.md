## Workstreams

- **AI strategy and operating models.** Adoption roadmaps, capability mapping, and operating-model design that turn strategy into shipped systems.
- **Responsible AI and governance.** EU AI Act alignment, risk classification, transparency, and governance without bureaucracy.
- **AI sovereignty and organizational capability.** Architectures and operating decisions that keep critical AI capability in-house and adaptable.
- **Agentic systems, RAG, and automation.** Retrieval-augmented and tool-using systems, MCP integrations, evaluation, and reliability engineering.
- **Public innovation and non-dilutive opportunity intelligence.** EU tender and grant pathways, consortia design, and opportunity intelligence.
- **Applied AI transformation.** Areas of work include healthcare, digital twins, public-sector innovation, and enterprise transformation.
- **Executive AI advisory and implementation leadership.** Board- and C-suite-level decision support; embedded technical leadership where it ships.

## How we work

- Outcome-first. No AI theater.
- Build what matters. Measure what changes.
- Speed and sovereignty over feature breadth.
- Strategy that becomes systems. Systems that learn.
